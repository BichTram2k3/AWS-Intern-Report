### Limitations
Despite the positive outcomes, I recognize several areas that need improvement:
* Firstly, adapting to the cloud environment was initially challenging, especially in understanding and configuring components such as EC2, RDS, VPC, and Security Groups to ensure system stability.
* Secondly, I encountered multiple technical issues during implementation, including database connection errors, charset configuration problems, and missing drivers. Resolving these issues required extensive research and repeated testing.
* Thirdly, my system design mindset was initially limited, as I focused more on functionality rather than optimizing cost, security, and scalability.
* In addition, my technical communication and teamwork skills still need improvement, as I worked mostly independently during the internship. My ability to read and fully comprehend technical English documentation also needs further development to better adapt to new technologies.
