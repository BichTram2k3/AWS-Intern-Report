### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment was friendly, open, and supportive. Members of the First Cloud Journey (FCJ) program were always willing to help when I encountered difficulties, even outside of working hours. The workspace was well-organized and comfortable, which helped me maintain focus and productivity. However, organizing more team bonding or networking activities could further strengthen connections among participants.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
* The mentor provided clear and detailed guidance throughout the internship. Whenever I faced difficulties, explanations were given in a structured and understandable way. I highly appreciate that the mentor encouraged independent problem-solving rather than simply providing answers, which helped me develop critical thinking and debugging skills.
* The admin team was also very supportive in handling procedures, providing materials, and ensuring a smooth working process.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The internship and AWS project were highly relevant to my major in Information Technology. Through this experience, I gained practical knowledge in deploying web applications on cloud infrastructure, including services such as EC2, VPC, RDS, S3, and IAM. This helped bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world application.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
The internship provided valuable opportunities to enhance both technical and problem-solving skills. Challenges faced during implementation, such as system deployment, debugging, and optimization, significantly improved my analytical thinking. More importantly, I realized the importance of continuous learning and adapting to new technologies in the rapidly evolving IT field.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The organizational culture was positive and professional. Team members respected each other and maintained a balance between productivity and a supportive atmosphere. During high-pressure situations, everyone collaborated and supported one another regardless of role, which helped me feel included as part of the team even as an intern.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The company provided internship allowances and offered flexible working conditions when necessary. Additionally, participating in internal training sessions and technical workshops was a valuable benefit that contributed significantly to my learning experience.

### Suggestions & Expectations

- For the University: I recommend that the faculty integrate more cloud computing courses and hands-on labs (especially AWS-related) into the curriculum. Organizing seminars, workshops, or guest lectures with industry professionals would also help students better understand real-world requirements and reduce the gap between academic knowledge and industry practice.

- For the Company / Internship Program: The internship program provided significant value in both technical and professional development. To further enhance its effectiveness, I suggest expanding advanced training sessions on topics such as Infrastructure as Code (IaC), including tools like Terraform or AWS CloudFormation.
In addition, providing more opportunities for interns to participate in the full lifecycle of real-world projects—from design to deployment and optimization—would greatly improve practical skills and problem-solving abilities.
